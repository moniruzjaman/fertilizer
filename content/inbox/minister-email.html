Honourable Minister,
Assalamu Alaikum. Adab.

With profound respect, I submit for your kind consideration a ministerial action plan to complete Bangladesh's fertilizer journey. The nation today holds 1.365 million tonnes of fertilizer in government warehouses, with a further 365,000 tonnes of approved imports in the pipeline. Supply is no longer our constraint — the opportunity now lies in distribution excellence, institutional integrity and precise subsidy targeting.

The attached briefing recommends a transition from the present blanket, dealer-centric model to a biometrically authenticated, land-linked, digitally tracked system — ensuring the right farmer receives the right input, at the right price, at the right time. Efficiency recovery alone is projected to return Tk 2,000–3,000 crore to the exchequer every year, allowing the reform to pay for itself within two to three fiscal cycles.

Transition to a biometric, land-linked, digitally tracked distribution system. Seven actions below require your signature — none requires new legislation, and each unlocks the next within a single budget cycle.

Seven Actions for the Honourable Minister
মাননীয় মন্ত্রীর অবিলম্ব বিবেচনার জন্য সুপারিশ
1
Issue the biometric PoS circular
Direct the Secretary to mandate biometric point-of-sale piloting in all divisions within 90 days, using Krishak Card and NID as the identity backbone.
2
Link stock allocation to land data
Instruct BADC & DAE to integrate allocation with the BDS/EDLMS land-parcel database, so quotas are land-linked by the upcoming Boro season.
3
Champion the 2026 Dealer Policy with confident enforcement
Empower the Secretary to reward compliant dealers swiftly and apply predictable, fair consequences for unlifted stocks or sales above MRP.
4
Approve the e-voucher pilot in two districts
Request the Finance Division to move subsidy delivery from companies to farmers over mobile financial service rails.
5
Establish the independent oversight committee
Farmer leaders, civil society and technical experts publishing quarterly transparency reports — institutionalizing shared stewardship.
6
Deliver one million Soil Health Cards in 18 months
Task SRDI, starting in the most fertilizer-intensive rice belts, so entitlements rest on soil evidence.
7
Approve the KrishiChain BD pilot — US$219,450
Commence immediately in Chattogram & Khulna; Phase-2 funding contingent on a 95% transaction-capture rate.

Honourable Minister, the technology is ready, the policy framework exists, and the regional evidence is clear. A warehouse full of fertilizer becomes a nation full of nourishment the moment every farmer's hands are filled. I humbly seek your directive to set this plan in motion.

With the deepest respect and gratitude,
